A personal website can start simply: a few words of introduction, one project and something you have just learned. Its value grows as you add more notes.

## Start with the small things

You do not have to wait for a major achievement before writing. A bug you finally understood, a clearer explanation or an open question can all be starting points.

## Keep a record of the journey

Notes help you look back on how your thinking has changed. A post does not have to answer every question; it just needs to record something useful you have learned.
