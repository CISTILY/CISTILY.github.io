## Start with a specific problem

Who will use the product? What do they need to accomplish? A clear answer helps you choose the first feature.

## Experiment step by step

1. Build the smallest usable version.
2. Try it with real data.
3. Record what could be better and improve one part at a time.

## Record what you learned

At the end, write down the decisions, challenges and lessons. These notes help you reflect and give others a clearer understanding of the project.
